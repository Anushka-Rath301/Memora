#What is Memora?
An AI-powered photo detective that finds your face in a sea of event photos before your patience runs out. It finds your pictures so you don't have to spend hours zooming into random faces.

##PROBLEM
Event organizers often share hundreds or even thousands of photos through Google Drive links or shared albums.
Finding your own photos means:
-Endless scrolling
-Wasting hours
-Missing photos altogether

##Solution
Memora aims to:

-Accept a few reference photos (selfies)
-Scan large event photo collections
-Identify photos containing the user
-Create a personalized album automatically

###Planned Features
 -Face Detection
 -Google Drive Integration
 -Automated Album Creation
 -AI Agent Workflow
